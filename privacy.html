Privacy Policy

How Sufra collects, uses, and protects customer information.

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Sufra respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using the Sufra platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sufra collects from customers, why we collect it, how it is used, and how it may be shared when you make a reservation.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a reservation through Sufra, we currently collect:

  • Customer name
  • Customer phone number

We also create and retain information associated with your reservation, such as the listing you reserved, the quantity reserved, the reservation status, pickup information, and reservation time.

Customers do not currently need to create an account to make a reservation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use customer information to:

  • Create and manage reservations.
  • Allow vendors to identify customers when fulfilling a reservation.
  • Provide the information necessary for pickup.
  • Contact you when reasonably necessary regarding your reservation.
  • Maintain reservation records.
  • Detect and prevent repeated misuse of the reservation system.
  • Maintain records of repeated no-shows where necessary to protect vendors and reduce wasted surplus food.

We intend to use personal information for legitimate purposes connected with operating and improving the Sufra reservation service.

3. Information Available to Vendors

When you make a reservation, the vendor responsible for that listing may receive information necessary to fulfil the reservation, including your:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Reservation details

Vendors should use this information only for purposes connected with fulfilling and managing the reservation.

4. No-Show and Reservation Protection

Sufra allows vendors to manually mark a reservation as a no-show when a customer fails to collect a reservation.

Repeated no-shows may be recorded against the phone number used for reservations.

This information may be used to assess reservation reliability and, where the applicable Sufra rules are met, temporarily restrict future reservations.

A no-show record does not automatically mean that a customer is permanently prohibited from using Sufra.

5. Cash Payments

Sufra currently supports cash payment for reservations.

Sufra does not collect or store customers' card or bank-payment information as part of the current reservation process.

Payment is made directly according to the terms shown for the applicable reservation.

6. Data Retention

Sufra currently retains reservation and related customer information for operational and record-keeping purposes, including reservation management, pickup records, dispute handling, and prevention of repeated misuse of the reservation system. The specific retention period for customer information has not yet been finalized. Sufra may establish and update a defined retention period as the service develops and its legal and operational requirements are further determined. When information is no longer required for legitimate operational,legal, or security purposes, Sufra intends to handle it in accordance with applicable requirements.

When personal information is no longer reasonably necessary for these purposes, it may be deleted or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Data Security

Sufra takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information in circumstances where deletion is permitted.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests may be subject to verification so that Sufra can protect customer information from unauthorized requests.

9. Third-Party Service Providers

Sufra may use third-party technology and infrastructure providers to operate the platform and store or process information.

Where third-party providers process personal information on Sufra's behalf, such processing is intended to be limited to purposes necessary for providing and operating the service and to be subject to appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Sufra may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the platform, our data practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have a privacy-related request or question, Sufra will provide an appropriate contact method through the platform.

A dedicated privacy contact email will be added to this section when available.

12. Applicable Law

Sufra intends to handle personal information in accordance with applicable laws and regulations governing personal-data protection in the State of Qatar, including Qatar's Personal Data Privacy Protection Law No. 13 of 2016, as applicable to the service.

This Privacy Policy describes Sufra's current practices and is not a substitute for legal advice.
